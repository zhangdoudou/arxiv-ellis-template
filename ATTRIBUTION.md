# Attribution and changes

This template adapts the LaTeX layout in the TeX source of:

Lin Chen, Bolin Ni, Qi Yang, Lan Jiang, Kun Ding, Xiaoran Fan, Hower Yang,
Ying Wang, and Shiming Xiang. *How Far Are We from Removing the Visual Encoder?
Scaling Laws for Encoder-Free Multimodal Pretraining*. arXiv:2609.35457,
September 28, 2026.

- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35457
- Source: https://arxiv.org/src/2609.35457
- Listed license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The source's `report.sty` is a modification of the NeurIPS 2024 style file
(`neurips_2024.sty`) by Roman Garnett and the authors of `nips15submit_e.sty`.
That provenance is retained here; this adaptation is not an official NeurIPS style.

Changes: renamed the style as `arxiv-ellis.sty`; preserved the single-column
page geometry, font, section, title and first-page header settings; replaced the
logo with the ELLIS Institute Finland logo; moved common packages and hyperlink colors into the style; removed the anonymous
submission mode, line numbers, paper checklist macros and stale conference
strings; replaced all paper-specific material with generic examples; added
build documentation.

`assets/ellis-institute-finland-logo.png` is the official ELLIS Institute
Finland horizontal logo. ELLIS names and marks remain the property of their
owners. The content license does not grant trademark rights or imply endorsement.
The original Tencent HY logo from the source archive is not included.
